How to play

The kingdom’s Royal Ledger is corrupted. Explore, talk to people, and open the rune doors by answering SQL questions about the kingdom’s data.

SQL terminals

Read the task, then type a query, or tap the pieces to build it. Press Run (or Ctrl + Enter) to see your results. If they match what the rune wants, it glows: press Complete ✓. Extra columns are fine as long as the answer’s there. Stuck? A hint appears after two wrong tries, or straight away with a hint scroll. Leave keeps what you’ve typed for next time.

The Royal Ledger has four tables: Villagers (villager_id, name, job, house_id, age, coins), Houses (house_id, street, colour), Items (item_id, name, owner_id) and Monsters (monster_id, kind, room, hp).

Fighting

Bugs wander about: two hits with the Debugger defeats one. NULL ghosts drift towards you and flicker: you can only hit them, and they can only hurt you, while they’re solid. Defeated enemies often drop keywords, and come back if you leave the room. If you run out of hearts, you wake up at your last Ledger Stone.

Bram’s shop (Village Square)

  • Lantern of EXPLAIN (15): shows thin walls and hidden passages.
  • Healing tea (3): refills your hearts.
  • Hint scroll (5): shows a terminal’s hint straight away.
  • Sharper Debugger (25): bugs in one hit, NULL ghosts in two.
  • Heart container (30): one more heart.

Saving

The game saves itself as you go. Touching a Ledger Stone also saves and refills your hearts. Saves are kept in this browser on this device; on the title screen, Continue picks up where you left off.
